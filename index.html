Privacy Policy for Alarm Clock

Last updated: April 10, 2026

Alarm Clock is provided by Orange Orm. This app is designed to work offline and does not collect, track, store, or share personal data on external servers.

Data Collection

We do not collect personal information, analytics data, advertising identifiers, or usage data from the app.

Local Data on Your Device

Alarm settings, schedules, app preferences, and optional custom alarm recordings are stored locally on your device only.

Device Permissions

The app may request access to device features such as alarms, notifications, local storage, and microphone access for custom alarm recordings. These are used only to provide app functionality on your device.

Third Parties

We do not use third-party advertising trackers. We do not sell your data or share personal data with third parties.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact alarm@orange-orm.io.
