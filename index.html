Privacy Policy

Vekkerklokke med helligdager / Alarm Clock is provided by Lars-Erik Roald. This policy describes how the Android and iOS app handles data. Alarms and holiday calculations work offline. Optional Pro purchases use Google Play or Apple's App Store and RevenueCat, which process purchase-related data outside your device.

Accounts and Advertising

You do not need to create an app account or sign in. The app does not display ads or request advertising identifiers. We do not sell your personal data or use purchase information for advertising.

Local Data on Your Device

Your alarms, schedules, alarm groups, holiday settings, custom days off, app preferences, and optional alarm sound recordings are stored on your device. The app does not upload these contents to the developer or RevenueCat.

Depending on your device and backup settings, app data may be included in backups managed by Android or iOS, including cloud backups. These backups are controlled by your operating system and backup provider, not by an app account with us.

Purchases and RevenueCat

The app uses RevenueCat to check, unlock, and restore Pro access. RevenueCat receives purchase history, such as product and transaction identifiers and purchase status, together with a randomly generated app user ID. This ID links the app installation to its purchase information; it is not a name, email address, or account you create. The SDK also sends technical context, such as the app version, operating system, platform, and locale.

This information supports purchase validation and restoration, Pro functionality, and purchase and revenue analytics. RevenueCat can be contacted when the app starts to check access or load purchase options, even if you do not make a purchase. There is no separate in-app switch to disable this purchase-service processing.

Google Play or Apple handles payment. The app does not receive your full payment card details or bank account details. RevenueCat processes purchase-related data as our service provider. Data sent to RevenueCat is encrypted in transit. See RevenueCat's privacy policy, Google's privacy policy, and Apple's privacy policy for more information about these providers.

Device Permissions

Alarm, notification, and full-screen alarm permissions allow the app to ring and show alarm controls, including when the device is locked. Microphone access is used when you choose to record a custom alarm sound. These recordings stay in app storage and are not sent to RevenueCat. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, or personal calendar to calculate holidays.

Retention and Deletion

You can delete alarms and recordings using the app's controls. Removing the app and its data removes local app storage; copies in operating-system backups may remain until removed through your backup settings.

Purchase records are retained by RevenueCat to support ongoing Pro access, purchase validation, and restoration. They are not automatically deleted when you uninstall the app, and we have not configured automatic deletion within 90 days. There is currently no in-app control for deleting RevenueCat purchase records. Google Play and Apple retain their own transaction records under their respective policies. Removing local app data does not delete those records.

Support Correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and retain the correspondence as needed to handle and follow up on the issue. Do not send passwords or full payment card details.

Contact
